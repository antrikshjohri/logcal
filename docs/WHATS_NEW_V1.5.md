# What's New in LogCal v1.5

## What's New

    ### Fix a meal right after you log it
    Something off? Tell the app what to change and it **updates that meal** so your totals and history stay right.

    ### Better when you talk
    Speaking what you ate is **clearer and more reliable** before you hit log.

    ### Smoother logging
    Your **summary after logging** shows macros in line with your totals, can **clear on its own** after a while, and picking a **new date** on the calendar takes **one tap**.

    ### Better when you use a photo
    Estimates **pay more attention to your picture** when you log with text and an image together.

    ### Dashboard and history
    **Today’s calories** and **macro rings** are easier to read. In **History**, each meal is **tidier**, and tapping a meal **opens edit** so you can change things in fewer steps.
