# What's New in LogCal v1.7

## What's New

### Favorite Meals
You can now save frequently logged meals to your Favourites. Rename, manage, and log them again in just one tap directly from the Log screen.

### App-Wide UI Revamp
Enjoy a premium forest-green redesign across all screens. Featuring custom cards, rounded typography, micro-macro indicator capsule pills, and polished goal status highlights.

### Universal iPad & Multitasking Support
LogCal is now a fully universal app! Experience a tailored iPad navigation sidebar, a double-column home dashboard, and auto-scaling layouts that adapt beautifully to Split View and Slide Over sizes.

### 50% Faster Performance
Optimized Cloud Services and ViewModels deliver drastically faster meal estimation responses and transcription speech recognition.

### Better History Tracking
The History view now expands your two most recent logging days by default, making reviewing and editing your recent entries completely seamless.
