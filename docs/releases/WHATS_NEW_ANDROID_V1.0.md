## 3. Play Store Full Description (4,000 Characters Max)

```text
Track what you eat effortlessly with LogCal — the intelligent calorie and macro tracker powered by advanced AI.

No more tedious manual database searches or measuring every gram. Just speak naturally, snap a photo of your plate, or send a quick message to log your meals in seconds.

KEY FEATURES:

🍽️ AI-Powered Food Logging
• Speak Naturally: Describe what you ate in your own words (e.g., "Two scrambled eggs with sourdough toast and an iced latte") and let our AI calculate calories, protein, carbs, fat, and fiber.
• Photo Recognition: Snap or upload a photo of your meal for instant visual nutritional analysis.
• Preview Mode: Curious about a dish before eating? Estimate nutrition without adding it to your daily log.

❤️ Google Health Connect Integration
• Sync logged calories and macros directly to Health Connect.
• Automatically import active calorie burn, steps, and basal metabolic rate to adjust your daily targets in real-time.

⚡ 1-Tap Favourites & Quick Logging
• Save your frequent meals and log them with a single tap.
• Customize serving sizes and portion multipliers on the fly.
• Continuous non-blocking logging lets you enter multiple meals in succession.

💬 WhatsApp Meal Logging
• Link your WhatsApp account to log meals on the go without even opening the app.
• Receive instant calorie estimates and daily summaries right inside your chat.

📊 Daily Goals & Progress Tracking
• Set customized calorie goals or use our built-in Diet Style Helper (Balanced, High Protein, Keto, Low Carb).
• Track your intake with clean visual charts, day-by-day history, and detailed itemized breakdowns.
• Beautiful dark and light modes tailored for modern Android devices.

🔒 Privacy & Security First
• Your data is encrypted in transit and securely synchronized with your private account.
• Full account and data deletion controls available directly in the app.

Start building healthier eating habits today with LogCal!
```
