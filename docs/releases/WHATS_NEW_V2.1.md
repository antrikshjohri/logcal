# What's New in LogCal v2.1

## What's New (App Store Release Notes)

### Lock Screen Complications
Access your calorie progress and log meals directly from your Lock Screen. We've introduced standard Accessory complications:
- **Circular & Inline**: View your remaining or overdue calories at a glance.
- **Rectangular**: See your current protein, carbs, fat, and fiber totals in a neat grid.
- **Shortcuts**: Instantly launch directly into the meal scanner (camera) or hands-free voice log with dedicated launcher complications.

### Dynamic Calorie Status
Lock Screen widgets dynamically transition from "left" to "overdue" with matching accent warning colors the moment you exceed your daily target.

### Visual Detail Overlays
Enjoy a better history view. Tap on any meal image thumbnail to open a sleek, full-screen zoom overlay that respects iOS safe areas and includes a simple top-right close action.

### Micro-Layout Refinements
We optimized macro preview pills across the home dashboard and log views. They now support automatic font tightening and scaling, preventing truncation of labels on smaller screens.
