# What's New in LogCal v2.1

## What's New (App Store Release Notes)

### Lock Screen & Home Screen Widgets
Add widgets to your Lock Screen or Home Screen to see your daily calorie progress at a glance, and use quick shortcuts to log new meals instantly.

### Meal History Photos
View local photos of your logged meals directly inside your history log. Tap any photo thumbnail to open a full-screen view and inspect details.

### Bug Fixes & Performance
General performance improvements, visual polishing, and bug fixes to keep your logging experience fast and smooth.
