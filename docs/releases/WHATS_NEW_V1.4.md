# What's New in LogCal v1.4

## What's New

### Macros Tracking
Track protein, carbs, and fat alongside calories. View your daily macro breakdown on the dashboard with progress rings, see macros per meal in history and meal details, and get a quick summary in the success card after logging.

### Better Cloud Sync
All your meal logs now sync reliably to the cloud, so your data is complete whether you log with text, photos, or both.

### Bug Fixes
Removed debug instrumentation for a cleaner production build.
