# What's New in LogCal v2.3

## What's New (App Store Release Notes)

### Apple Watch Companion App
Log meals right from Apple Watch with voice logging, live sync, and a fast watch-first flow for reviewing today, recent meals, and favourites.

### Apple Health Integration
Connect Apple Health to sync calories and macros from logged meals, then use active energy and steps to keep your remaining calorie budget more accurate throughout the day.

### Customizable Dashboard
Choose which dashboard cards matter most to you, including calories, macros, weekly trends, streaks, and today's activity.

### Better Activity & Nutrition Charts
Weekly charts now support more nutrition views with smoother animations, cleaner labels, and clearer calorie/activity summaries.

### UI Polish & Bug Fixes
Improved text fitting across History, Home, and Dashboard, refined Apple Watch complications, clarified surplus messaging, and tightened meal title handling.
