# LogCalAI Website Redesign Brief

## Product
LogCalAI is an AI calorie tracker where users can log food by typing, speaking, or uploading a meal photo.

## Main Promise
Calorie tracking should feel effortless, not like manual data entry.

## Target User
Busy people who want to stay fit, lose weight, or be mindful of calories but hate logging every ingredient manually.

## Positioning
Log meals in seconds with AI.

## Homepage Goal
Drive users to download the app.

Primary CTA: Download the app  
Secondary CTA: See how it works

## Brand Direction
Premium, modern, calm, health-tech, trustworthy, Apple-level clean.

## Avoid
Generic SaaS look, too many gradients, fake placeholder cards, stock imagery, excessive animation, too much text.

## Visual Direction

Use a clean, light, premium design system.

Preferred visual style:

- Soft off-white / cream background
- Light green or warm neutral tones
- Deep green, charcoal, or near-black primary text
- Rounded cards
- Large elegant headline
- Strong whitespace
- High-quality spacing
- Subtle shadows
- Subtle gradients only where useful
- Polished phone mockups
- Clean app preview sections
- Mobile-first responsive design

Avoid:

- Too many gradients
- Neon colors
- Crypto-style AI visuals
- Generic blue SaaS look
- Random abstract blobs
- Fake-looking placeholder cards
- Stock photos
- Excessive animations
- Too much text
- Too many icons
- Cluttered sections

## Homepage Sections

Build the homepage in this top-to-bottom order:

### 1. Hero
Purpose: Explain what LogCalAI does within 5 seconds.

Suggested copy:
- Headline: Log meals in seconds with AI
- Subheadline: Tell LogCal what you ate. It estimates calories and keeps your day on track.
- Primary CTA: Download the app
- Secondary CTA: See how it works

Visual:
Use a polished phone mockup or app preview.

### 2. Problem
Purpose: Show why normal calorie tracking is painful.

Message:
Traditional calorie tracking feels like homework. Users have to search foods, measure portions, enter ingredients, and repeat it every day.

### 3. How it works
Purpose: Make the app feel effortless.

Use 3 steps:
- Speak it: Say what you ate naturally.
- Snap it: Upload a meal photo.
- Track it: LogCal estimates calories and updates your day.

### 4. Features
Purpose: Show what the app can do.

Include:
- AI meal logging
- Voice food logging
- Photo-based calorie estimates
- Text-based meal entry
- Daily calorie dashboard
- Meal history
- Streaks or consistency tracking

### 5. Product mockups
Purpose: Make the product feel real.

Show polished mobile app mockups for:
- Home dashboard
- Meal logging screen
- Daily progress
- Meal history

If real screenshots are unavailable, create realistic placeholder screens that look production-ready.

### 6. Benefits
Purpose: Explain why users will care.

Focus on:
- Less time logging
- Easier consistency
- Better calorie awareness
- Works for real meals and messy plates
- Helps users stay mindful without obsessing

### 7. Final CTA
Purpose: Push users to act.

Suggested copy:
- Headline: Start tracking smarter today.
- Subheadline: Your next meal can be logged in seconds.
- CTA: Download the app

### 8. Footer
Include:
- LogCalAI name/logo
- One-line description
- Privacy
- Terms
- Contact/Support
- App Store link placeholder
